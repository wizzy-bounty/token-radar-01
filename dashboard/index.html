Out-of-sample evidence — the model, tested before it is believed

Every candidate term had to improve all four held-out tests of the ridge estimator at once — leave-one-out, walk-forward (earlier listings price later ones), leave-one-family-out, leave-one-chain-out — or it was refused, however good it looked in-sample. The shipped consensus is then scored under the same four tests, coefficients refit in every split. Miss = exp(median |log error|): the typical prediction is off by that factor.
