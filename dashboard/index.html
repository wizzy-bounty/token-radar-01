RADAR-8 BREAK-EVEN FIRST

OUT-OF-SAMPLE EVIDENCE — every term, four held-out tests

A term is kept only if it lowers the miss in at least 3 of 4 tests: leave-one-out (LOO), walk-forward (earlier listings price later ones), leave-one-family-out (LOFO), leave-one-chain-out (LOCO). The control term — the 7-day growth of the next protocol in slug order — must not help, and is never kept.

Value model — typical miss, x-fold (lower is better)

Odds model — Brier score (lower is better)

THE 758-PROTOCOL LEDGER — every protocol accounted for once

Verdicts recompute live from the scenario. Default: 6% drop share, 26 weeks, T-bill 4%/yr, hands-on time at $25/h.

RADAR-8 prices each row at its own minimum viable capital: the smallest deposit whose net — drop share of implied value, pro-rata to TVL, minus plan fees and hands-on hours at $25/h — clears a 4% T-bill over the same horizon. FARM: viable capital exists and is at most $250,000. MARGINAL: net positive somewhere but under the T-bill line, or viable only above $250,000. SKIP: no deposit in the domain breaks even. Implied value is this board's own claim, never a prediction that a token will be issued.
